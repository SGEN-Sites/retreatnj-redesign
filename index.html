Who We Are

Wellness – reimagined.

We work tirelessly to make self-care better – for all people. As a woman-owned and operated adult-use dispensary, The Retreat embraces that a ‘retreat’ is unique to each human. It is to that end that we walk beside you in your individual pursuit to wellness.

Our StoryMeet Natalie Benson
656 Grand StBergen-Lafayette, Jersey City

Inclusivity

We believe in wellbeing – for all people. From accessible purchasing options to quality, local product selections and community initiatives, we’re mindful of creating an oasis for all across Hudson County.

Ease

Our main priority is to create a space that is your “retreat.” Whether that’s our tranquil interior atmosphere, our seamless customer service experience, or our no-nonsense ordering process, The Retreat is all about letting you be at ease, at all times.

Balance

There is hustle and bustle, and then, there is rest and recovery. We urge you to allow yourself the luxury of indulging in yourself, of finding your personal retreat, and settling in.
