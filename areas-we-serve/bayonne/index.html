Visit · Bayonne

A dispensary near Bayonne

We’re just down the way from Bayonne, an easy stop on your way home from work or before a quiet night in.

Getting here

An easy stop after work

The Retreat sits at 656 Grand St in Jersey City’s Bergen-Lafayette neighborhood, near The Junction and just blocks from Liberty State Park and Berry Lane Park. We love being a part of the cannabis community in Jersey City, Bayonne, and surrounding areas.

If you ride the Light Rail, we’re a 5-minute walk from the Liberty State Park station. Driving? We recommend parking on State Street, where there are plenty of options, ranging from metered street parking to parking garages.

We’re open until 9 PM Monday to Saturday and until 8 PM on Sunday, so there is time to stop by after work.

After work in Bayonne

Ways to decompress

Rather than go home and drift asleep on the couch, our Bayonne guide suggests turning the post-work hours into a relaxing time that sets you up for a pleasant evening.

Take a long walk

Try the Richard A. Rutkowski Park Loop for a paved, boardwalk-style trail and great birding, or the Bayonne Bridge Walkway for gorgeous views of the bustling area.

Journal in the park

Stephen R. Gregg Park, the city’s largest, offers unbeatable views and plenty of walking paths. Dennis P. Collins Park has shaded benches, and it’s a great place to sit down with a journal and reflect.

Browse a bookshop

The Little Boho Bookshop is a quiet reprieve from the outside world. Pick out titles that catch your eye, read a few pages, and move at your own speed.

Pause and reflect

The Teardrop Memorial can be a place of calm and quiet reflection, followed by a peaceful stroll along the waterfront.

A night in

Choose for the evening you want

Before checking product aisles or online menus, think about what kind of vibe you’re looking for and what you’d like out of the experience. Cannabis affects everyone differently depending on the strain, potency, product type, and your own tolerance and body composition.

  • Flower and pre-rolls suit those who like the ritual of it all. Pre-rolls skip the grinder and rolling papers.
  • Vapes are a discreet option with a milder aroma than smoke.
  • Edibles are smoke-free and long-lasting, but can take 30 to 90 minutes to kick in. Start low and go slow.
  • Check the label and certificate of analysis for potency and the cannabinoid and terpene profile.

Your visit

From Bayonne, start to finish

  1. 01

    Order ahead

    Browse our online menu at your own pace and place an order for pickup.

  2. 02

    Bring your essentials

    A valid government-issued photo ID showing you’re 21 or older, plus cash or debit. There’s an ATM in store; credit cards aren’t accepted.

  3. 03

    Ask questions

    Tell your budtender what kind of evening you have in mind. Ours are extremely knowledgeable about cannabis in general and our unique selection.

  4. 04

    Enjoy it at home

    Keep products sealed until you reach a private residence. Public consumption is not legal in Jersey City, and never drive after consuming.

Bayonne questions

Coming from Bayonne

Is there parking near The Retreat?

Yes. We recommend checking out State Street. Whether you’re coming from Bayonne, Hoboken, or elsewhere, there are plenty of parking options available, including metered street parking and nearby parking garages.

What is the closest Light Rail station?

Liberty State Park. We’re a 5-minute walk from the Liberty State Park Light Rail station.

How late are you open?

Until 9 PM Monday to Saturday, and until 8 PM on Sunday. We open at 10 AM Monday to Saturday and at 11 AM on Sunday.

Do I need a medical card?

No. You don’t need a medical marijuana card to buy recreational cannabis in New Jersey. Simply provide proof of age with your government-issued ID.
