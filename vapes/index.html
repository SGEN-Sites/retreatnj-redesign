Vapes at The Retreat

Flavor, control and discretion

Vaping heats cannabis oil to a specific temperature, releasing its active compounds as a vapor rather than smoke. Because nothing is burned, flavor comes through clearly: vape cartridges are formulated specifically to allow for nuanced flavors and aromas to come through.

Vapes are discreet, too. Vaping does not produce as much of an odor as smoking, especially at lower temperatures, and the aroma it leaves tends to be milder. We personally vet all of our brands, so you can shop with confidence.

Vaping vs. smoking

Is there a difference?

Everyone wants a concrete answer on which is better. The truth is, it depends on the experience you want to design for yourself.

How it works

Smoking burns the flower, and you inhale what’s released. Vaping heats cannabis to a set temperature and releases the active compounds as a vapor, inhaled through a mouthpiece.

Control and onset

Vaping allows for greater control over the experience. Smoking can be less precise in terms of dosage, and you’ll likely feel the effects more quickly and more intensely.

Flavor and aroma

For a flavor-forward experience, lean toward vaping. With smoke, the flavor of terpenes tends to fade more quickly or get lost in the smokiness.

Which suits you

If you’re a true beginner, smoking is simple and straightforward; if discretion matters, a vape is a smart choice. Given the existing research, there is no clear winner, so buy from reputable sources, like a licensed dispensary.

Questions

Vapes, answered

Do I need a battery for a cartridge?

Yes. A cartridge attaches to a separate battery, and most use a standard 510 thread. Disposables have the battery built in. If you’re unsure what fits, ask a budtender.

Are vapes good for beginners?

They can be. We recommend that you start low and take it slow, especially if you are a beginner. While vaping allows for more control, it is often better suited for people already familiar with cannabinoids and terpenes.

Which vape brands focus on flavor?

&Shine builds its vapes around rich terpene profiles, and Fernway is known for crisp, true-to-flavor profiles. Some products and brands don’t prioritize flavor, so ask your budtender before selecting a cartridge.

Can I order vapes ahead for pickup?

Yes. Browse our online menu and place an order for pickup right on our website, then pay in store with cash or debit; there’s an ATM inside. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID; you must be 21 or older.
