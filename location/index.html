Visit The Retreat

Find a unique experience of ‘retreat’

The Retreat is located near The Junction, just blocks from Liberty State Park and Berry Lane Park. We’re the perfect wellness stop on your way home or after a day outdoors.

We’re a 5-minute walk from the Liberty State Park Light Rail station and a few blocks away from Berry Lane Park.

Getting here

Parking and directions

Parking

State Street

We recommend parking on State Street – there are plenty of options available, ranging from metered street parking to parking garages.

7 mins

From Downtown Jersey City

Head south on Marin Blvd, then turn right onto Grand St.

12 mins

From Hoboken

Take Observer Hwy to Marin Blvd, then follow to Grand St.

18 mins

From Newark

Use I-280 E and NJ-139 E to Jersey City; take the Grand St exit.
