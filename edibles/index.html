Edibles at The Retreat

A smoke-free ritual

Not everyone likes to smoke, and edibles are one of the simplest ways to enjoy cannabis without it. Our menu carries gummies, chocolate bars and fruit chews from brands known for flavor and consistency, from tailored terpene blends to precise, predictable servings.

Edibles also work differently. Because of the digestive process, edibles take much longer to settle in and can have a more potent effect. Knowing that one thing makes for a calmer, more comfortable experience, and it’s why we always recommend starting low and taking it slow.

Before your first bite

  • Start with a low dose
  • Be patient; effects take time
  • Wait a few hours before taking more
  • Store edibles sealed, away from children and pets

Start low, go slow

Why edibles take longer

When you smoke or vape, effects tend to arrive quickly. Edibles are digested and processed through your liver, which means it can take between 30 and 90 minutes to feel them, and the effects can last longer too.

  1. 01

    Start with a low dose

    Begin with the lowest dose. Our Hoboken weekend guide suggests starting with half, or even a quarter, of an edible.

  2. 02

    Be patient

    If you don’t feel anything right away, that’s normal. Settle in with a playlist, a good book or a favorite movie while it takes effect.

  3. 03

    Wait before taking more

    Only increase your dosage after a few hours. Taking more too soon can leave you with more than you intended.

  4. 04

    Note what you enjoyed

    Remember the product, the amount and how you felt. Next time, you and your budtender have a clear place to start.

How to choose

Finding the right edible

Look for low-dose options

For smoke-free options, Camino, Wana and Incredibles are celebrated for their micro-dosing and consistency. It’s easy to start with a gentle, predictable amount.

Choose an easy format

Gummies are simple to portion, and Incredibles’ gummies and scored candy bars let you break off exactly what feels right.

Think about timing

Wana’s fast-acting gummies take the guesswork out of timing. Ask a budtender how a product is made to work before you plan an evening around it.

Let flavor lead

Camino crafts each variety around a specific mood with tailored terpene blends, and Good Tide brings a natural, tropical escape to your routine. Taste is part of the ritual.

Questions

Edibles, answered

How long do edibles take to work?

Longer than smoking or vaping. Because of the digestive process, edibles take much longer to settle in and can have a more potent effect; it can take between 30 and 90 minutes to feel them. Be patient, and wait a few hours before taking more.

Which edibles are good for beginners?

Camino, Wana and Incredibles are celebrated for their micro-dosing and consistency. Camino’s low-dose gummies are made for a gentle experience, and Incredibles’ scored candy bars let you break off just a little.

Does New Jersey limit how much I can buy?

Yes. New Jersey limits how much cannabis you can buy in each transaction, and edibles count toward that limit. Budtenders keep track of it for you at the counter. Learn more in our guide to New Jersey cannabis laws.

Can I order edibles ahead for pickup?

Yes. You can browse our online menu and even place an order for pickup right on our website. We take cash and debit cards, and there’s an ATM inside our store. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID; you must be 21 or older.
