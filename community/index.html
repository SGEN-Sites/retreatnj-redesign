Community partner

Building a Stronger Jersey City Together

A true retreat is something we create together. It’s a shared commitment to caring for one another, supporting our neighbors, and protecting the community around us. At The Retreat, we believe a space of rest and renewal can only exist when people look out for each other.

That’s why we’re proud to be a community partner in Jersey City, actively supporting initiatives that make a difference right where we live, work, and play. Our work aims to continue Jersey City’s spirit as a welcoming retreat for people of all nationalities, creeds, and walks of life.

Our work

How We Make a Difference

At The Retreat, we’re our community’s “Golden Door”, investing in local programs, hyperlocal employment, and partnerships that strengthen the city at large. By building relationships beyond our customers, we support neighborhood growth, wellness, and opportunities for all Jersey City residents.

01

Social Equity Programs

Helping people returning from incarceration get IDs and birth certificates to access jobs, housing, and opportunities.

02

Community Partnerships

Partnering with local organizations on cleanups, toy drives, and neighborhood wellness initiatives.

03

Local Brand Support

Prioritizing local cultivators and brands while building meaningful relationships with farmers and operators.

04

Inclusive Hiring Practices

Recruiting locally and supporting second-chance employment opportunities through community reentry programs.

05

Cannabis Advocacy & Education

Promoting safe, regulated cannabis through education, product transparency, and advocacy for stronger industry standards.

Local brand support

Curating Cannabis with Care and Community

We support local, women-owned, and national cannabis brands at our Jersey City dispensary, partnering with nearby cultivators like Hillview Farms to provide traceable, high-quality products. We build strong relationships with farmers and operators, ensuring every product meets rigorous safety standards.

Hiring

Inclusive Hiring & Social Equity

At The Retreat, we believe everyone deserves a chance to make a difference. Our inclusive hiring practices prioritize hyperlocal employment, recruit from the immediate community, and provide second-chance opportunities through SCORES Reentry, ensuring a diverse, equitable workforce that truly reflects Jersey City.

Whether you’re a non-profit, community organization, local business, or neighborhood leader with opportunities to support community initiatives, programs, or events, we’d love to hear from you and explore ways to make a meaningful impact together.
