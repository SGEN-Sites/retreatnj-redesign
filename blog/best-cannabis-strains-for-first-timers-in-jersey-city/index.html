Best Cannabis Strains for First-Timers in Jersey City

3 min read · The Retreat

Dabbling in the world of adult-use cannabis can be thrilling but also overwhelming and confusing. Most local markets in Jersey City are full of cannabis options, and finding your footing and choosing the right products for you is easier when you know what you’re looking for.

Whether you’re a local, a tourist, or visiting from Bayonne or another neighboring city, going in and checking out NJ dispensaries should be an enjoyable, relaxed, and educational experience, which is what we always aim for at The Retreat! Stop by our chill and friendly dispensary in Jersey for some of the best cannabis products and tailored recommendations from our budtenders for newcomers.

But if you’d like to brush up beforehand, we’ve put together a guide on what to look for and included some beginner-friendly, top-tier strains found in our best pre-rolls for an easy and fun first-time experience.

Tips for Newcomers Buying Recreational Cannabis

Before getting into specific strains and things to look for in packaging and labels, keep these golden tips and tricks in mind when you’re shopping in a dispensary in New Jersey:

  • Go Low & Slow: Look for strains with low to moderate THC percentages (ideally under 20%, but this can vary) or options with a balanced cannabinoid profile where CBD and terpenes offer a mellow and smooth experience.
  • Consult Budtenders: Budtenders are your anchors in a dispensary. You can lean on them to help and guide you toward the best products for you. Don’t forget to mention you’re new to cannabis!
  • Follow the Law: Recreational cannabis is legal only in private spaces, so don’t step out of a Jersey dispensary and light up on the street, as public consumption remains illegal.

Best Beginner-Friendly Strains at The Retreat

Dogwalkers – Jack’s Delight “Play” Big Dog Pre-Roll

Jack’s Delight by Dogwalkers is the perfect blend of energetic, vibrant, and chill. This blend of Jack Herer and Point Break makes this pre-roll a sativa-dominant dream of uplifting vibes, perfect after lunch or in social settings.

Jack’s Delight stands out for its inviting blend of flavors and aromas. Dominated by sweet, citrus, and earthy notes, the strain offers a sensory experience that can be as enjoyable as its effects. This uplifting yet cool effect profile makes this a great recommendation for newcomers who don’t want a very strong and psychoactive high.

Papaya Crush Boardwalkers Pre-Roll

Papaya Crush by Boardwalkers is a potent cross of Bolo Runtz and Papaya that’s an Indica-heavy hybrid. One of the strongest products on this list, Papaya Crush has a tropical fruit candy, ripe papaya, and earthy aroma and flavor profile.

This pre-roll delivers a quick wave of euphoria, elevating your mood. After a few minutes, the mood boost is followed by a deep, tingly, and smooth body relaxation that doesn’t make you locked to the couch. Best for daytime relaxation, creative work, stress-busting, or sweet-flavored cannabis consumption.

Glass Meadows – Honey Banana

A balanced hybrid of Strawberry Banana and Honey Boo Boo strains, Honey Banana by Glass Meadows offers a well-rounded and uplifting high that makes you feel inspired and giggly in the beginning and eases into a soothing and comfortable body high with mild sedative effects.

A sweet green banana-like flavor mixed with rich honey, along with tropical fruity and light earthy undertones, makes for a well-rounded aroma and flavor profile. Great for mid-day inspiration, social unwinding, and relaxation.

Glass Meadows: Slurricane

Bred as a cross of the strains Do-Si-Dos and Purple Punch, Slurricane by Glass Meadows is an indica-dominant pre-roll that has a heavy-hitting fruit profile full of sweet berries, grapes, and citrusy notes, rounded out by spice and peppery notes.

The Slurricane has a slow-building and immersive body high that reduces tension, stimulates appetite, and melts away physical discomfort and pain. It’s best suited for evening wind-down routines, combating sleeplessness and providing deep physical relaxation.

Stop by The Retreat for the Best Beginner Cannabis Products

Embarking on your cannabis journey doesn’t have to be overwhelming or confusing. Whether you’re looking to unwind, get the creative juices flowing, get a mood boost for socializing, or just need some extra relaxation on the weekend, The Retreat’s inviting dispensary with the perfect cannabis product options is waiting for you!

While this guide focuses on pre-rolls since they’re beginner-friendly, check out our online menu and familiarise yourself with potency, flavors, and different cannabis product types like vapes, edibles, and flower. Stop by our Jersey City dispensary today and chat with our budtenders to get custom product recommendations that work for your lifestyle and preferences!
