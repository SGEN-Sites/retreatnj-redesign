A Beginner’s Guide to Visiting a Dispensary in Jersey City

3 min read · The Retreat

Whether you’re just starting your cannabis journey or are looking for a new go-to shop in Jersey City, you may have a few questions.

Ahead of our grand opening, The Retreat team is here to answer them with our guide to visiting a dispensary in Jersey City! After all, we’re all about equipping you with the knowledge and products you need to create a space that is your own “retreat.” Let’s settle in…

What You Need to Visit Our Dispensary in Jersey City

Knowledge is power. Here’s what to know before you go to an adult-use cannabis dispensary like ours in Jersey City.

What to Bring to The Retreat

New Jersey state regulations require a valid government-issued photo ID (21+) to purchase cannabis from an adult-use dispensary. Not a local? Take a deep breath…it’s not a problem! Here at The Retreat, we will welcome visitors from out of state with a valid 21+ ID as well.

Payment Options at The Retreat

Our goal is always to make your cannabis shopping experience as soothing and seamless as possible. That’s why at The Retreat, we will offer the flexibility to pay with cash or debit card. We’ll also have an ATM on site for your convenience. Unfortunately, due to industry regulations, we will be unable to accept credit cards.

Tips for a Smooth Dispensary Visit in Jersey City

Want to make your upcoming trip to The Retreat even more relaxing? Keep these tips in mind:

  • Order online. Upon opening, you’ll be able to browse our online menu at your own pace, and even place an order for pickup right on our website. Reserve your favorite products ahead of time to skip the line and make sure your favorites are in stock. Then, come on in for relaxed, seamless pickup and payment!
  • Ask questions. Our budtenders at The Retreat are extremely knowledgeable, not only about all things cannabis, but also about our unique selections. You’re always welcome to speak one-on-one with them about your specific needs, so you can find the products that bring you to your own retreat.
  • Double-check your essentials. Make sure you have a valid ID and payment form with you before heading out to The Retreat.
  • Park with confidence. If you’re looking for parking near The Retreat, we recommend State Street. There are plenty of options available with both metered street spots and nearby parking garages.
  • Know your source. Not all dispensaries carry the same products and brands. At The Retreat, our inventory is stocked with social equity brands, mission-driven products, and more. Support high-quality, ethical cannabis when you shop with us in Jersey City!

Looking for more tips or resources along your cannabis journey? Check out our dispensary FAQs and blog posts now.

Exploring Cannabis Products at Jersey City’s Favorite New Dispensary

One of the things we’re most proud of at The Retreat is our wide range of premium products from top local brands. Our team has been working hard to curate this collection for you, including:

  • Flower: The tried and true classic.
  • Edibles: A tasty and discreet option.
  • Pre-rolls: Convenient and ideal for a shareable sesh.
  • Concentrates: A pure, potent choice for connoisseurs.
  • Vapes: Full flavor, on-the-go.
  • Topicals: Soothe sore muscles and dry skin.
  • Accessories: To help you spark up in style.
  • And much more!

We Invite You to Visit The Retreat in Jersey City, Opening Soon!

We’re changing the way cannabis is done to bring wellness to the forefront of the conversation. From our warm and welcoming shopping spaces to our high-quality product selection, we invite you to create your own retreat while you’re here.

We can’t wait to meet you when our dispensary location opens soon in Jersey City! Stay tuned for the launch of our online menu, and reach out to us with any questions you may have in the meantime.
