Value, your way

Getting the most from every visit

A tip from our own guide: check out the menu beforehand. If you’re looking to have a premium experience, purchase high-end cannabis products on sale or get the ones just below the highest tier to get the most value out of your money.

Looking for everyday value? Legend offers flower and shatter as everyday staples, with exceptional value without skipping on quality. For quality without the premium price tag, Simply Herb’s vape cartridges, flower and shake are crowd-pleasers.

Or take it from a local’s Google review: “They have great deals and a large selection.”

Questions

Offers, answered

Where can I see current offers?

Check our live menu, where you can learn more about deals, offers and product drops. It’s worth a look before every visit.

How do I hear about new products?

Join our email list, which shares exclusive offers on our favorite new products, or follow us on Instagram at @theretreatnj.

Can I reserve something I saw on the menu?

Yes. Place an order for pickup right on our website to skip the line and make sure your favorites are in stock, then pay when you pick up.

How can I pay?

We take cash and debit cards, and there’s an ATM inside our store. Unfortunately, due to industry regulations, we’re unable to accept credit cards at this time.

Before you visit

Check the menu, then come say hello

Browse what’s in stock, order ahead for pickup and pay in store with cash or debit. We’re at 656 Grand St, open Monday to Saturday 10 AM to 9 PM and Sunday 11 AM to 8 PM.
